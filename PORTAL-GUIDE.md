# Using the school portal

Sign in, then choose a large task button on Home. Other functions remain under More tools. The same guidance is available in English and French.

## Principal

Home shows class lists, staff accounts, marks, document requests, classes and subjects, and teacher assignments. Under Set the academic year, enter a year such as 2026/2027 and select Set year for everyone. This sets the working year for all users. Earlier records are retained. A new year starts with mark entry closed unless its entry window was previously opened.

## Principal and vice-principal

On Home or Marks and report cards, choose an assessment and Open or Closed, then save the entry setting. Open one sequence or all assessments. Closing entry prevents new marks and score changes. Existing results remain readable, and administrators may publish or withdraw existing marks without changing scores.

## Teachers and heads of department

Home shows Class lists, Take attendance, Marks and report cards, View timetable, and your assigned classes and subjects. The school year is set by the principal.

For attendance, choose your lesson and date, tick Present, Absent, Late or Excused for every student in the grid, and save. Mark all present is available as a shortcut. The grid loads the register already saved for that lesson and date. For marks, check that entry is open, select Add record, choose a class and student, enter the mark, and save. The student and subject choices follow your assigned classes. If entry is closed, ask the principal or vice-principal to open it. Closing and reopening a page does not lose previously saved records.

## Other roles

Home shows the main tasks for your role. Students see lessons and homework, results, timetable and profile. Parents see results, attendance and timetable. Discipline staff see attendance, class lists and summaries. Website editors see school news and their profile. All existing role permissions still apply.

## Getting help

If a task fails, read the message shown near the form. If the school year or entry setting has changed, refresh the page before trying again. Do not share your password. Change a temporary password when first signing in.

## Attendance summaries

Teachers can choose Attendance summaries on the Attendance page or School summaries on Home. Choose a date range, class and subject to see rates, presence, absence, late and excused counts. Teacher reports contain only their assigned subjects and classes for the shared year.

The principal, VP and discipline staff see school-wide attendance reports. Designated administrative accounts (including secretary, bursar, stores accountant, guidance counsellor and ICT coordinator) see aggregate attendance summaries without individual pupil names or marks. They do not receive additional access to individual registers. Present and late count as attendance. Excused absences are reported separately and excluded from the percentage. Missing registers are not counted as absences.
