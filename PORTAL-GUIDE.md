# Using the school portal

Sign in, then choose a large task button on Home. Other functions remain under More tools. The same guidance is available in English and French.

## Principal

Home shows class lists, staff accounts, marks, document requests, classes and subjects, and teacher assignments. Under Set the academic year, select a year from the dropdown and review any advancement before confirming. This sets the working year for all users. Earlier records are retained. A new year starts with mark entry closed unless its entry window was previously opened.

## Principal and vice-principal

On Home or Marks and report cards, choose an assessment and Open or Closed, then save the entry setting. Open one sequence or all assessments. Closing entry prevents new marks and score changes. Existing results remain readable, and administrators may publish or withdraw existing marks without changing scores.

## Teachers and heads of department

Home shows Class lists, Take attendance, Marks and report cards, View timetable, and your assigned classes and subjects. The school year is set by the principal.

For attendance, choose your lesson and date, tick Present, Absent, Late or Excused for every student in the grid, and save. Mark all present is available as a shortcut. The grid loads the register already saved for that lesson and date. For marks, check that entry is open, choose your assigned class and subject, enter the marks in the class grid, and save. The student and subject choices follow your assigned classes. If entry is closed, ask the principal or vice-principal to open it. Closing and reopening a page does not lose previously saved records.

## Other roles

Home shows the main tasks for your role. Students see lessons and homework, results, timetable and profile. Parents see results, attendance and timetable. Discipline staff see attendance, class lists and summaries. Website editors see school news and their profile. All existing role permissions still apply.

## Getting help

If a task fails, read the message shown near the form. If the school year or entry setting has changed, refresh the page before trying again. Do not share your password. Change a temporary password when first signing in.

## Attendance summaries

Teachers can choose Attendance summaries on the Attendance page or School summaries on Home. Choose a date range, class and subject to see rates, presence, absence, late and excused counts. Teacher reports contain only their assigned subjects and classes for the shared year.

The principal, VP and discipline staff see school-wide attendance reports. Designated administrative accounts (including secretary, bursar, stores accountant, guidance counsellor and ICT coordinator) see aggregate attendance summaries without individual pupil names or marks. They do not receive additional access to individual registers. Present and late count as attendance. Excused absences are reported separately and excluded from the percentage. Missing registers are not counted as absences.


## Annual student advancement

The principal selects the next academic year from the Home dropdown and reviews the proposed class movements before confirming. The dropdown always includes the next three future years and adds another future year as the current year advances. Advance one year at a time.

Forms 1 to 4 move to the next form with the same class suffix. Form 5 moves to Lower Sixth using its configured Arts or Science stream. Lower Sixth moves to Upper Sixth in the same stream. Upper Sixth completes school, stays in the historical records, leaves the new-year class lists and loses active student portal access. Dismissed, transferred-out, graduated and unassigned records are not advanced. Existing destination-year enrolments and unclear classes are held for review.

All updates and the shared academic year are applied in one transaction. A stale preview is rejected, and each year can be advanced only once. Mark entry starts closed in the new year. Earlier attendance, marks and class history are retained. Selecting an earlier or already-advanced year changes the viewing year without repeating advancement.

## Email and phone notifications

On Home, open Email and phone notifications. The principal connects the school Gmail using its Gmail address and a 16-letter App Password. Enable Google 2-Step Verification and create the App Password in the school Google account. Enter it only in the portal, never in a message. The portal verifies the SMTP connection before saving encrypted credentials. Existing Gmail mailbox contents are not accessed.

Each user enters and verifies their own notification email with the emailed code. They can switch email notices off. Phone notices require an explicit Allow phone notifications action and browser permission on each device. On iPhone or iPad, add the portal to the Home Screen and open that installed app. Notification support depends on the browser and device. Use Send test notification to check delivery.

The principal can compose a school notice for teachers, parents, students or other staff, with class and stream filters for students and parents. Review the recipient count before sending. Delivery goes only to active school records whose users enabled a verified email or registered push device. The result reports sent, disabled or failed channels. These are manually sent notices, not automatic absence or results alerts. No email delivery is available until the school Gmail is connected.

## Public department guides

Under Academics, select a department for its purpose, study areas, expected competencies, learning activities, assessment and progression. Guides are available in English and French. Exact class content follows approved MINESEC and GCE syllabuses and school schemes of work. Progression resources link to the MINESEC Inspectorate service with the displayed academic year. Download availability depends on the source publishing data for that subject and class.
